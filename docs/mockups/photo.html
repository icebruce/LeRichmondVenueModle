Photo mode

A camera button opens Photo mode from the toolbar (desktop) or the top-right stack (phone). Plans › “Save this view as an image” opens the same mode. Inside, everything but the room and one bar is hidden. You can still frame the shot by dragging, and a tap on the room sets the focus. Save image renders the view at full quality and downloads it; Close returns to the same view.

New Dashed outline marks what is new on each screen.

Behaviour

Situation What happens
Editing while in Photo mode Photo mode is a layer on top of the room. The selection is paused and every edit shortcut is off: Delete, Undo / Redo, R and the 1 / 2 / 3 cameras. Escape, Close and the phone's back gesture only close Photo mode, and closing brings the selection back.
Plan view, comparing The camera button is disabled, with the label “Photo mode works in Dollhouse and Walk”, as the light is in Plan. Comparing disables it too.
Walk, Sit here The walk pad and the seated bar are hidden while the photo bar shows. Close returns to the same spot or seat.
The image fails (memory, lost graphics) One retry at screen size. If that fails too, the toast reads “Couldn't make the image. Try again, or set blur to Off.” and Save image comes back. Close while making the image cancels it.
Phones on Battery saver The blur previews only once the view is still; the saved image is the same.
Keyboard and screen readers The room takes focus: Enter focuses the centre, the arrow keys move the focus ring, and a status line says “Focus set”. Before any tap, the centre is the focus, so the default works without a pointer.

For the owner: the saved image shows the room only, so labels such as “Photo preview” on imported items are not in it (already the case for today's saved view). Keep it that way, or add a small footnote on the image naming any approximate items?

What Photo mode changes in the picture

While framing The saved image
The device's own graphics tier while the view moves, then the full-quality frame once it is still. Full quality on every device: High shading, soft shadows, live ambient occlusion, and the detailed chairs, flowers and tableware.
Candles and lamps glow as they do now. Candles and table lamps also light the cloth and the décor around them.
The blur previews at screen size. Depth of field around the focus point, at the chosen blur.
— Twice the screen's size on a desktop (a 1440 × 900 window gives 2880 × 1800); the screen's own pixels on a phone, up to 2048 px on the long side.
