## Research discipline

- For each reference, record likely camera location, frame direction, architectural landmarks, date, confidence, and possible temporary decorations.
- Do not infer floor size from the number of tables or from marketed maximum guest capacity.
- Use in-room scale cues and cross-view geometry for a plausible **parameterized estimate**; retain unknowns with ranges.
- A linked photo on a venue page may be copyrighted; use as private research reference. Source photos should not be used as publicly distributed app assets without permission.
- Screenshots user brings later may improve estimates. The first 3D build must proceed without requiring them.
